# UX16 decisions: the kudos drawer at the bottom of the board

The owner asked for the original Retroscope's kudos panel back: a tab at the bottom centre of the retro board that
opens a drawer from the bottom, toggled with the N key. Since U36 decision 7, kudos had been a toolbar button
(`Kudos N left`) that opened a centred dialog. UX16 changes the client only. The server, the protocol, the model,
`kudosOf`, the give and revoke commands, the nacks, the live updates and the celebration stay as they were.

UX16 adds `web/src/retro/keyboard.js`, `tests/web-unit/retro/keyboard.test.js`,
`tests/web-unit/poker/keyboard.test.js` and this file. It edits `web/src/retro/{kudos,board}.js`,
`web/src/poker/keyboard.js`, `web/src/core/a11y.js`, `web/src/ui/popover.js`, `web/styles/{retro,kit}.css`,
`tools/quick.mjs`, the unit tests `core/{a11y,ui}.test.js`, the specs `retro-actions/{kudos,roster}.spec.js` and
`real/retro-team.spec.js`, and the screenshot states in `tests/e2e/screenshots/capture.spec.js`.

## Decisions

1. **"Kudos", not "Endorse".** The tab reads `Kudos`, then the count left as a badge, then a `<kbd>N</kbd>` hint.
   "Kudos" is the word used by the team page and its leaderboard. The accessible name stays `Kudos N left`, so
   screen readers announce the same thing as before. The button carries `aria-keyshortcuts="N"` and
   `aria-haspopup="dialog"`. The hint is an `aria-hidden` sibling of the button, laid over the button's right
   padding with `pointer-events: none`, so a click on it still reaches the button. It is not inside the button
   because axe's `label-content-name-mismatch` compares the visible text, aria-hidden text included, with the
   name: "Kudos 3 N" is not contained in "Kudos 3 left" (the rule UF16 fixed for this button). Under
   `@media (pointer: coarse)` the hint is hidden, because a phone has no N key to press.
2. **The tab lives in a new `end` slot.** `createBoard` now passes an `end` slot (an empty `display: contents`
   element after the column row) beside `toolbar`, `top` and `before` (U34 contract 4). The tab is fixed, so
   where it sits in the DOM only sets its place in the tab order. At the end of the board, the keyboard reaches
   it after the columns, which is where it appears on screen. The fixed element is `.retro-kudos-dock` (the
   button and the hint), and it is the one that is `hidden`. The button's class changed from
   `.retro-kudos-button` to `.retro-kudos-tab`, and the specs follow.
3. **The drawer is still `createDialog`, styled as a bottom sheet.** `className: 'retro-kudos'` and rules in
   `retro.css` make it full width, hung from the bottom edge (`margin: auto auto 0`), with rounded top corners, a
   primary-tinted top border, `max-height: 80dvh` with scrolling, a grab handle, a blurred backdrop and a 300 ms
   slide-up (the kit's `sheet-up` keyframe in `kit.css`, beside the side sheet's `sheet-in`). `dialog.js` did not
   need a change. A native modal dialog keeps what the original lacked: a focus trap,
   an inert background, Escape, closing on a backdrop click, and focus back on the tab. The title stays
   `Give kudos`, so the dialog's accessible name is unchanged, and it gets the award icon. The original's header
   badge ("N remaining") is the existing status line `You have N of M kudos left on this board.`, centred above
   the cards. The specs read that line. The base `prefers-reduced-motion` rule in `base.css` cuts the slide to
   0.01 ms. Closing is not animated: a native dialog leaves the top layer at once, and a slide-down would need a
   delayed `close()` that fights Escape and focus return.

   The drawer closes whenever the tab hides, whatever the reason: the viewer leaves the roster, or a fresh
   snapshot has no live kudos type left. Focus goes back to the tab only while the tab is visible.
4. **Padding, not margins, inside the sheet.** `createDialog` treats a click whose target is the `<dialog>` itself
   as a backdrop click. A click on the dialog's own padding or on a child's margin has that target too. The sheet
   has no padding of its own. Its header, description and body use inline padding that centres them in a
   64rem-wide band, so a click on empty space beside the cards does not close it. A click on the grab handle (a
   `::before` on the dialog) does close it, as a backdrop click would.
5. **One card per teammate, with the type names kept.** The cards wrap in a centred row, each 10rem to 13rem
   wide, which gives two per row on a phone. A card shows an avatar, the name, and one full-width toggle per live
   type. The original's buttons showed only an emoji, with the name in a hover tooltip. That does not work on
   touch screens, and the e2e specs find each toggle by its type name. So each toggle keeps today's content: the
   emoji, the name (cut with an ellipsis when a count badge needs the room) and the count badge. The
   `aria-pressed` toggles, the `Kudos for {name}` groups, the disabling at the limit and the `title` tooltips are
   as before. A team with no other members shows "No other team members to give kudos to."
6. **The N key.** `keyboard.js` exports a pure `togglesKudos(event, {openDialogs, drawer})` and
   `bindKudosKey(toggle, drawer)`, modelled on `poker/keyboard.js`. Each of the following is ignored:
   - Ctrl, Meta, Alt or Shift held;
   - a repeated key, an IME composition, or an event that is already `defaultPrevented`;
   - a target inside an input, textarea, select or contenteditable (not `contenteditable="false"`);
   - any open `<dialog>` other than the kudos drawer;
   - any open popover or menu. `createPopover` surfaces are not `<dialog>`s, so the guard asks
     `popoverOpen(doc)` in `ui/popover.js`, which looks for a `.popover` without `hidden`: a closed popover stays
     in the page with `hidden`, and `destroy()` removes it.

   While the drawer is open, N closes it even with the focus on one of its toggles. Shift is ignored, as in
   poker. Caps Lock still works, because it sends `N` without Shift. The listener is bound only while the tab is
   shown (a team board whose viewer is a member, with at least one live type). It is unbound when the tab hides
   (for example when `retro.members` drops the viewer) and when the board unmounts.

   The text-field check is shared with the poker table's shortcuts as `isTypingTarget` in `core/a11y.js`, and
   `poker/keyboard.js` now also stands down while a popover or menu is open, which it did not before: N there
   is "next round", and a digit is a vote, so neither should fire behind an open round or seat menu.
7. **The tab never covers the end of a column.** While the tab shows,
   `.retro-board:has(.retro-kudos-dock:not([hidden]))` pads the board's bottom by 3.5rem plus the safe-area inset.
   Scrolled to the end, the last card, the composer and "Add card" all sit above the tab. `html` gets the same
   `scroll-padding-bottom`, so focusing a control scrolls it clear of the tab. Mid-scroll the tab still covers
   about 160 × 38 px of the bottom centre, as in the original. A spec checks the end-of-scroll case on every
   project. Without the padding it fails on mobile-webkit, where the board's own padding is only 0.5rem. The tab
   is at `z-index: 30`, below the mention list (50), the celebration (60), popovers and toasts. The retro timer
   is in the toolbar, so no other retro UI is fixed at the bottom. The toaster is fixed at the bottom right. On a
   phone it is nearly full width, so a toast covers the tab while it shows, which is the right way round.

8. **A celebration shows over the open drawer.** The modal drawer makes the rest of the page inert and blurs it,
   so a celebration appended to `body` would be hidden under the backdrop and could not be clicked away.
   `celebrate(details, host)` appends to the drawer's `<dialog>` while it is open, and returns
   `{close, moveTo(host)}`: opening the drawer moves a live celebration into it, and closing it moves the
   celebration back to `body`. Both are `position: fixed`, so they sit in the same place either way. During the
   drawer's 300 ms slide-up the dialog has a transform, so a celebration moved in at that moment is placed
   against the sheet until the slide ends.

## Open

- **Real visual baselines.** `real/visual.spec.js` captures `.retro-columns` on a team board, so the tab is on
  screen. On the fake board at the same viewports, the column row ends at y = 368 (1280 × 900) and y = 394
  (390 × 844), and the tab starts at y = 862 and y = 806. The tab is fixed outside the row's box, and the bottom
  padding is on `.retro-board`, not on `.retro-columns`. So the baselines should not change. They were not run:
  real e2e is out of scope for this unit.
- `real/retro-team.spec.js` now uses `.retro-kudos-tab`. It was updated but not run, for the same reason.
- `real/a11y.spec.js` scans a team board, so it scans the tab. It was not run either. A one-off axe scan with the
  same tags against the fake board, with the drawer closed and open, in light and dark on Chromium, found no
  violations.
