# U36 decisions: retro action items and kudos

U36 owns `web/src/retro/{actions,prev-actions,kudos}.js` and `tests/e2e/fake/retro-actions/**`. All three plug
into the board through U34's feature slots (`docs/decisions/U34.md` contract 4).

## Contracts later units rely on

1. **Features.** `actions.js` exports `actionItems` (`board` + `card`), `prev-actions.js` exports
   `previousActionItems` (`board`, `before` slot) and `kudos.js` exports `kudos` (`board`, `toolbar` slot).
   `index.js` lists them in that order: `[actionItems, previousActionItems, kudos]`.
2. **The team link (`teamLink(ctx)` in `actions.js`)** is the board page's subscription to `team:<teamId>`
   (§c.2 "board pages of team members subscribe to both rooms"). One link per board `ctx`, reference-counted
   by `use()`. It subscribes only while the board view has `teamId` and the viewer is in `view.members`
   (the board projects the roster to members only, S12), and it unsubscribes when `retro.members` drops the
   viewer. It uses the team model of `web/src/team/model.js` and sends assign/done with that model's
   optimistic intents. Team nacks are toasted by the link.
3. **The tab's socket.** The team room is joined on `getSocket()` from `screens/app.js`, the tab's one
   socket, rather than on the board's socket: U34's feature `ctx` does not carry the socket and `board.js`
   is not U36's. Once UF11 passes `{socket: getSocket()}` to the retro `mount()`, both rooms share one
   connection. Until then the board opens its own socket (U34 contract 1) and the team room rides on the
   entry's socket, which `entry.js` already starts on `/retro/` paths.
4. **Jira config** for "Create in Jira" comes from `GET /api/teams/{id}` (`jira.configured`, `jira.siteHost`,
   `jira.membersCanEdit`, U30's shape), fetched once per team and only when an action-items card with a team
   item is on screen. The create is `POST /api/teams/{id}/jira/issues {summary, description}` (§f), answered
   `{key}`. **U26:** keep `{key}` in the 2xx answer of `POST issues`.

## Decisions where the spec was silent or did not fit

1. **Which cards get the controls.** A card in the action-items column shows assignee, done and "Create in
   Jira" once the team room holds an item with `sourceCardId == card.id`. Between the card's tx and the
   team room's `team.action.added` (or for a non-member, always) it shows none of them.
2. **Assignee is a menu button, not a `<select>`** (WebKit's `<select>` trips the CSP's `style-src-attr`,
   U34 decision 2). "Nobody" sends `userId: null`. An assignee no longer in the roster shows as "Former
   member".
3. **Assign and done are allowed on archived boards and in every stage.** They are team-owned rows (§c.2),
   not board content, and the team page offers them at any time; the TeamDecider has no stage gate.
4. **Summary for Jira** is the card's first non-empty line of plain text (mentions as `@name`, whitespace
   collapsed), cut to 255 characters with an ellipsis; the description is the card's markup, which the server
   converts with `MarkupToAdf`. A created key is kept for the page view only (no column stores it) and
   replaces the button with a link to `https://{siteHost}/browse/{key}`. The button is hidden unless Jira is
   configured and the viewer may write (`membersCanEdit`, or admin/owner).
5. **Previous action items.** The column lists the team room's items that are open and not sourced from this
   board (items whose board was deleted included), oldest first. An item marked done from this page stays in
   the column, shown as done, until the page is reloaded, so "Mark done" can be undone in place;
   the count badge counts open items only. The column is attached to the `before` slot only while it has
   items, so an empty column never shows. When it first appears it scrolls the row back to the start,
   because scroll snapping otherwise keeps the previously first column in view and hides the new one.
6. **Previous items' comments** are read with `GET /api/teams/{id}/action-items/{itemId}/comments` when the
   thread opens and refetched whenever `team.action.comments` bumps the item's revision (U30 decision 3).
   Posting is `POST … {commentId, body}` (never anonymous: the team route has no such field); a refused
   post goes back into the composer. Images go to `/api/retro/{sourceBoardId}/images`, the board the comment
   lands on, through the composer's `onError` mapping (409/413/415/507). Drafts use
   `rs.draft:<boardId>:action-comment:<itemId>`.
7. **Kudos drawer is a dialog**, opened from a toolbar button that shows how many kudos are left. One row per
   teammate, one toggle button per live (not archived) type; pressed means "I gave this", and clicking a
   pressed one revokes it. Buttons for new kudos are disabled at the limit, which follows `team.settings`
   live. Give and revoke are optimistic; a nack rolls back and is toasted (`endorse_limit` has its own
   sentence). The button counts received kudos per type on this board.
8. **Celebration** runs when `state.celebrate` changes to an endorsement addressed to the viewer (U34
   contract 3), so a re-sent `added` for the same id does not replay it, and a snapshot never does. It shows
   a status card for 4 s (click closes it), 24 confetti squares positioned through the CSSOM and animated
   with Web Animations (no CSS keyframes needed, nothing for the CSP to refuse; skipped under
   `prefers-reduced-motion`), and a four-note WebAudio arpeggio (C5 E5 G5 C6, triangle wave). Audio failures
   (no user gesture yet) are ignored.
9. **Members only.** Without `members` in the board view (non-members and guests on `link`/`password` team
   boards, S12) there is no kudos button, no previous-items column, no action-item controls, no `team:`
   subscription and no `/api/teams/…` request. `roster.spec.js` checks this, including the case where a
   `retro.members` frame drops the viewer.

## Follow-ups for other units

- **U34 / UF11 (`retro.css`):** the features reuse U34's classes (`retro-column` with `data-color="yellow"`
  for the previous-items column, `retro-card`, `retro-card-meta`, `retro-cards`, `retro-comments`) and kit
  classes, and have no stylesheet of their own. Worth styling: `.retro-card-done` (dim or strike the body),
  `.retro-action-item` (the controls row), `.retro-jira-key`, `.retro-kudos-*` and `.retro-celebration`
  (its position is set through the CSSOM today).
- **U34:** a card slot for a full-width row would let `actions.js` stop moving its row from the action row
  (`card.js`'s `extraHost`) to just after `.retro-card-meta`; the action row does not wrap and has no room
  for three more controls.
- **UF11:** `tests/e2e/fake/retro-board/cards.spec.js` "the boot snapshot paints the board before the socket
  answers" fails on main as well: the entry's own socket (`getSocket()`) sends the first `hello`, with no
  subs, before the board's socket does. Passing `{socket}` to `mount()` fixes it.
- **U26:** `POST /api/teams/{id}/jira/issues` must accept `{summary, description}` from a team member and
  answer `{key}` (the fake does).
