# UX18 decisions: team-first navigation, members-only access

The owner found the navigation messy: a Dashboard that mostly repeated the team page, a Teams page and a team
dropdown that led to the same place, and poker and retros behind a tab next to Members and Kudos. Most people are
in exactly one team. After comparing three prototypes (top-bar team tabs, a team sidebar, one team page without
tabs), the owner chose the top-bar tabs and fixed these decisions:

- The app header becomes the team context: logo, team switcher, then the team's sections as tabs.
- The Dashboard and the Teams list are removed. A signed-in visit to `/` goes to the user's last team.
- Quick (team-less) poker sessions and retro boards are removed as a feature.
- Guest and signed-out access is removed. Everything that belongs to a team needs a signed-in member of that team:
  no guests, no `link` or `password` board visibility, no non-member access to team boards.
- A signed-in user in no team lands on a "create or join a team" screen.
- The change ships as **one release**, with no expand/contract split. One real team uses the app, and the owner
  accepts that production cannot be rolled back past this release (see Deploy below).

This narrows DESIGN §0.1 (guests and quick sessions were parity features with the original). It drops no core
feature: poker with the Jira refinement flow, retro boards, kudos and team action items are unchanged for members.

## Decisions

1. **Who may see what.** A signed-out visitor can reach only `/`, `/login`, `/auth/*` and `/invite`. Every other
   shell route answers a no-store `302` to `/login?return=<path and query>`, decided in `Shell` before the boot
   contributors run, so no room lookup is spent on a caller who can never be admitted. Every team resource (team
   room, poker sessions, retro boards, action items, kudos, members, settings, Jira, blobs on boards) needs a
   signed-in user who is a member. App admins keep the audited admin Teams tool (names, member counts, membership
   repair) and nothing else; there is still no admin bypass to team or room content.
2. **Missing and forbidden look the same.** The resolver and the team routes already answer a missing and a
   forbidden resource alike (S9). The client shows one screen for both, "You can't open this page", with "Back to
   <last team>" and "Use a different account". A member who is removed while a room is open sees "You no longer
   have access". No screen names the team or room a non-member asked for. A limit is not lost access: a room open
   in more of the user's tabs than `ConnectionsPerPrincipal` (6, §c.7) is kicked with its own code,
   `too_many_connections`, instead of the `forbidden` U13 decision 8 chose, so "You no longer have access" stays
   for `access_revoked` and `forbidden`. For it and for `too_many_rooms` (a tab with too many rooms open), the poker
   session, the board's banner and the team page say so in place and offer "Try again", which subscribes again.
3. **Guests are gone; their history stays.** `RoutePolicy.Guest`, `/api/guest` (`GuestEndpoints`), the guest
   cookie resolution and `guest_tokens` are removed. `/ws`, `GET /api/me`, the by-code lookups and timer music
   require a signed-in user (not a verified one: `aot-smoke` probes `/ws` as an unverified user). `BoardManage`
   and `SessionManage` require a user at the route filter. The `__Host-rs_guest` cookie is expired on sign-in and
   sign-out. Imported and earlier guest and Slack (`external`) principals are **kept**: their cards, votes, seats
   and readiness are history. Retro cards and comments keep their author's name and "guest" badge. On the poker
   table, a past voter who is not a current member shows as "Unknown" without a badge, because the poker snapshot
   carries `PrincipalInfo` only for the roster (the team's members) and the present principals, so it has none for
   them. This predates UX18 for team sessions (follow-up in `docs/followups/UX18.md`). `principals.kind` is
   unchanged. A former guest's open tab gets a 401 on its next socket upgrade, which is accepted (no shim).
4. **Quick rooms are gone.** `POST /api/poker` and `POST /api/retro` are removed with their rate limits and the
   quick-only branches in Core, the stores, the projections and the client. Existing team-less rooms are deleted by
   migration 0003 (with their `client_cursors` and `recent_activity` rows, which have no foreign key to the rooms).
   No archive: nobody could open it (tenet 4).
5. **Boards are team-only.** Board visibility, board passwords, `/api/retro/{id}/unlock`, access grants and the
   Share dialog's access options are removed. The Share dialog becomes "Copy link". `link` and `password` boards
   in production become ordinary team boards. If the room protocol or the board meta carries visibility, it is
   removed and the generated protocol files and golden transcripts are regenerated and reviewed. The board-access
   rule copied into SQL (unread-mention count, mentions list) becomes "member of the board's team".
6. **Dashboard, Teams list and favorites are gone.** `/dashboard` leaves `routes.js` and `ShellRoute.All` together
   and becomes a permanent no-store `302` to `/` (it is the installed app's old start URL and sits in bookmarks).
   `DashboardEndpoints` and `GET /api/dashboard` are removed; `ActivityRepo` moves to `TeamsEndpoints`.
   `GET /api/teams` (the list) and the favorite routes are removed, and `team_favorites` is dropped: it was
   personal data with no reader left. The cross-team "recent boards and sessions" go with the Dashboard, so
   `recent_activity` keeps only `team` rows. The manifest's `start_url` becomes `/` with `id: "/dashboard"`, so
   installed apps keep their identity.
7. **`/teams` is the create-or-join screen.** The path stays in the route table; `screens/teams.js` is rewritten.
   It offers "Create a team" (disabled with a reason and a resend link until the email is verified) and "Join a
   team": teams are invite-only, and a pasted invite (`…/invite#t=…`, `/invite/<token>` or a bare token) is
   previewed and opened. There is no team discovery and no request-to-join.
8. **`/` finds your team.** `me` gains `lastTeamId`: the most recent `recent_activity` row of kind `team` among the
   user's current memberships, else null. A team is touched (with the existing debounce) when the team page loads
   and when the user subscribes to any room of that team. The server answers a signed-in `/` with a no-store `302`
   to `/teams/<lastTeamId>`, else the first team in `me.teams`, else `/teams`, and the fake server answers the same
   way. The client applies the rule to in-app navigation, where a pending invite (decision 13) wins over the team.
   The server cannot see that invite, which waits in the browser's `localStorage`, and no cookie tells it: a full
   page load of `/` by a member (a reload, the installed app, the typed address) goes to their team, and the invite
   waits for the next landing that resumes it. Someone in no team lands on `/teams`, which resumes it.
9. **The header is the team.** Left to right: logo (team home), team switcher (the user's teams from `me.teams`,
   sorted by name case-insensitively, a check on the current one, the role under each name, then "Create a team"
   to `/teams`), the team's sections as links, then on the right the settings gear (admins and owners only), the
   mentions bell and the account menu. The account menu holds Account, Admin (app admins), a Light/Dark/System
   theme choice and Sign out; the separate theme button goes. The sections are `Poker` (default), `Retros`,
   `Action items` (with the open count), `Kudos` and `Members`, addressed as `/teams/:id?tab=poker|retros|actions|
   kudos|members` (`tab=boards` stays an alias of `retros`). Tab clicks push history; filters keep `replaceState`.
   The nav is labelled "<team name> sections" and marks the active tab with `aria-current="page"`. The team is
   taken from the route (`:id`, `:tid`), from a room's snapshot (`teamId`) on `/retro/:code` and `/poker/:code`,
   and from `lastTeamId` on pages without a team (account, mentions, admin), where the sections show with none
   active and no gear. On `/teams` the header shows only the logo, the product name and the account menu.
10. **Header data comes from `me`.** `me.teams[]` gains `openActions` (one grouped count over the open-items index,
    joined to the user's memberships). Pages that hold the team room patch the count, name and role live through a
    small header context API; the header never subscribes to a room. The client refreshes `me` after creating,
    joining, leaving or deleting a team, renaming it and changing roles, because a same-module navigation does not
    refetch boot. Header styles live in `kit.css`; the entry chunk is near its budget, so the header stays lean.
11. **The team page.** The in-page tab bar and the team header block go; the header owns the sections. Each tab
    has one `h1` and sits in a `main`. The admin Jira banner stays at the top of the page. Poker: "Start poker
    session", the latest session highlighted ("Latest session", pointed/total, "last activity N min ago", topics,
    Join), then earlier sessions. Nothing claims a session is live or in progress: sessions never close and no data
    says so. Retros: Active/Archived, "New board" (title and template only), a card per board with its title and
    date, plus its stage when stages are on. The list endpoints gain `lastActivityAt` (sessions and boards) and
    `stage`, `stagesEnabled` and `cardCount` (boards); they lose `visibility`. These are query-only additions.
12. **Rooms.** The retro board keeps the app header (Retros active, from the snapshot's `teamId`); the board's own
    header shows "<team> / Retros /" above its title, and the header no longer mirrors the board title. The poker
    screen keeps its own toolbar: the Back link becomes "<team> / Poker / <created date>", built from the snapshot's
    `teamId` (a session opened at `/poker/:code` or under another team's URL is `replaceState`d to
    `/teams/<teamId>/poker/<code>`), "Copy link" shows for every session, and the toolbar docks the mentions bell
    and the account menu. The date is the snapshot's `createdAt` (Unix milliseconds of `poker_sessions.created_at`),
    because imported sessions have ids that are no UUIDv7; the client falls back to the time in a UUIDv7 id, and then
    to the code, only for a snapshot without it.
13. **The invite is the way in.** `/invite` leads with "Email me a sign-in link" for a signed-out visitor: the
    magic link signs in, creates the account if needed and verifies the address, which an email invite requires.
    Google, Microsoft and password sign-in follow. The pending invite is kept in `localStorage` (`rs.invite`, with
    an expiry) and cleared on accept, on sign-out and when it expires. The landings after sign-in resume it. The
    magic-link consume and the verify "Continue" go to `/` in-app, where it wins over the team (decision 8). The
    sign-in card (the welcome page, `/login`) returns to `/invite` instead of `/` while it waits and no `?return=`
    was given, so its password forms land there, and so do Google and Microsoft, which come back by a full page
    load that the server would send to the team. The create-or-join screen at `/teams` opens it too, so a newcomer
    whose full page load of `/` the server sends there reaches it. A member's full page load of `/` goes to their
    team (decision 8).
14. **Resend verification.** `POST /api/auth/verify/request` (signed-in, unverified user; under `EmailBudget` and
    a per-user limit) re-sends the verification email. It answers the same way whether or not a mail was sent.
15. **Signed out, `/` is the welcome page.** `screens/landing.js` becomes a short product page with sign-in and
    sign-up (Google, Microsoft, email and password, the emailed sign-in link). The quick-start buttons, the guest
    name dialog and the join-by-code forms are removed.
16. **Kudos you received are team data.** `GET /api/me/endorsements` joins the user's current memberships, so the
    account page no longer shows kudos, endorsement-type names or endorser names from a team the user has left.
17. **Phone.** On team pages the header shows the team switcher and a horizontally scrolling row of the sections.
    The sheet keeps team switching, "Create a team", settings, theme and the account items, and loses the
    Dashboard and Teams links.

## Data: migration 0003

One migration, applied by the migrator at start-up; it must stay quick on the production database.

- Delete team-less poker sessions and retro boards (cascading to their rounds, votes, cards, comments and other
  rows with foreign keys), and the `client_cursors` and `recent_activity` rows that point at them.
- Set `team_id` `NOT NULL` on both room tables and drop the indexes that existed only for quick-room retention.
- Drop board visibility, the board password columns and `board_access_grants`.
- Drop `guest_tokens` and `team_favorites`.
- Delete `recent_activity` rows that are not of kind `team`, and narrow its kind check if it has one.
- Keep every principal, including `guest` and `external` ones.

`tests/schema.snapshot.sql` is regenerated (`RS_UPDATE_SCHEMA_SNAPSHOT=1`) and reviewed. The importer no longer
maps team-less source rooms (counted and reported as skipped) and writes team boards without visibility or
password columns; its goldens are regenerated (`RS_UPDATE_GOLDEN=1`) and reviewed. `RetentionJob` and
`TokenSweepJob` lose their quick-room and guest-token statements.

## Deploy

This release is not compatible with the previous one: the old binary would read the dropped columns and tables.
`deploy/RUNBOOK.md` §11 records that production is never rolled back past UX18, and that a backup is taken before
UX18 is merged to `main`, which deploys it; recovery from a failed deploy is a fix forward or a restore of that
backup. Anyone with the old app open is signed out of guest seats and must sign in; members' links keep working.

## Code and tests

- `Program.cs` loses `GuestEndpoints` and `DashboardEndpoints` (the owner agreed), together with
  `CompositionRootTests` (25 modules to 23) and the test hosts that compose those modules by hand.
  `GuestNames.IsPlainText` moves to a neutral helper first, in its own refactor commit.
- `Core/Authz` loses the quick, creator, grant and visibility arms. `tests/authz-matrix.csv` is regenerated
  (`RS_UPDATE_AUTHZ_MATRIX=1`) without the quick resources and the creator, guest-creator and grant viewers; the
  `guest` viewer stays as an all-deny row because legacy guest principals exist. Every capability keeps an allow
  and a deny row.
- Test fixtures default to team rooms and members. Golden transcripts that encode quick, guest or non-member
  access are replaced by team-member equivalents.
- The fake server, its demo and the fake e2e specs drop guests, quick rooms, board passwords, `/api/dashboard`
  and `/api/teams`, and serve the new `me` fields and list fields. The fake answers a signed-in full page load of
  `/` with the server's redirect (decision 8), so the specs see the team, not the pending invite, there.
- The real-stack e2e helpers, the post-deploy smoke suite, the soak run and the load and redeploy drills seed
  verified owners through the database and add other users through link invites. The `ws-idle` drill uses a
  pre-provisioned account passed through environment variables.
- `tools/quick.mjs` routes `Core/Authz` to the sim and the room, HTTP, blob, admin and hosting integration
  categories, and `routes.js` and `Server/Endpoints` to the hosting tests.
- The Linux visual baselines are regenerated once the UI settles. The macOS set needs the owner's machine.

## Not done

- No team discovery, request-to-join or public boards.
- No shim that tells a former guest's socket why it was refused.
- Board images already cached by a browser stay in its private cache after access ends; changing the year-long
  immutable caching would cost bandwidth on small hardware for little gain.
- `docs/DESIGN.html` has no generator and is stale until it is regenerated by hand.
