# UF14: front-end polish and one-socket rule (from wave 5)

UF14 owns `web/src/**` and `web/styles/**`, `web/budgets.json`, `tests/web-fake-server/**` (except `demo/**` data shapes, which it may extend), `tests/e2e/fake/**`, `tests/e2e/screenshots/**`, and `docs/decisions/{U31,U32,U33,U34,U35,U36,UF11}.md` (append-only). Units U32, U33, U35, U36 and UF11 are finished, so their files transfer to UF14.

1. **One socket per tab, always.** Team, poker and retro screens share the app-level socket from `entry.js`. Navigating team → poker → board → team never opens a second socket or reuses a client id. Add an e2e spec that walks those routes and asserts exactly one WebSocket and no 4409.
2. **Static files.** The plain fake server (not only the demo) serves the favicon, the manifest and robots.txt.
3. **Visual baselines.** Pin a timezone (`timezoneId: 'UTC'`) and locale in the Playwright config. Regenerate the baselines so they are stable across machines and OSes.
4. **Poker.**
   - Strip chips use the issue-type glyph from `ui/glyphs.js` instead of a tag icon.
   - The left Jira panel is resizable and persists its width, as the right panel does.
   - Bring the jira-drawer chunk comfortably under budget (at least 1 KB headroom) by trimming code, not by raising the budget.
5. **Retro.**
   - The settings dialog never sends a save it knows will be rejected; it shows the reason instead.
   - The timer resyncs its clock through the socket's own resync API, not a fake `online` event.
   - `focus.js` escapes ids with `CSS.escape`.
6. **CSP.** Audit for inline `style` attributes or `setAttribute('style', …)`. The CSP forbids inline styles; CSSOM property assignment is fine. Fix anything that could trigger the WebKit CSP violation seen in `team/actions.spec.js:159`.
7. **Owner review.** Review the cross-unit edits made at the wave 5 merge (`poker/index.js`, `retro/index.js`, `retro.css`, `board.js` in 78edcec) for coherence, and tidy anything left inconsistent.
