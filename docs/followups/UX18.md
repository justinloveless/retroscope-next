# UX18 follow-ups: team-first navigation and members-only access

`docs/decisions/UX18.md` removed guests, quick rooms, board visibility and passwords, the Dashboard, the Teams list
and favorites, and moved navigation into a team header. These items are left open.

1. **Regenerate the rendered docs.** UX18 changed `docs/DESIGN.md` (§0.1, §a, §b, §c, §d, §e, §f, §g.1, §g.2, §h, §j,
   §k and the critique rows S7, S9, S10, S12, S21 and R27) and `deploy/COOLIFY_SETUP.md` (sections 10.4, 11, 11.2,
   11.5, 14 and 15). `docs/DESIGN.html` and `deploy/COOLIFY_SETUP.html` do not have those changes, and the repository
   has no generator for them.
2. **Record the WebSocket idle check on production.** `deploy/RUNBOOK.md` section 15 now runs `ws-idle.mjs` with an
   account made for it beforehand (`RS_IDLE_EMAIL`, `RS_IDLE_PASSWORD`). Somebody has to create that account on
   production before the check can run.
3. **The macOS visual baselines** need the owner's machine. The Linux set is regenerated once the UI settles (UX18,
   "Code and tests").
4. **Earlier decision records** (U7, U8, U9, U11, U16, U21, U22, U25, U29, U31, U35, U41, UF11, UF12 and others) still
   describe guests, quick rooms and board visibility as they were built. They are history and are not rewritten;
   UX18 and `docs/DESIGN.md` describe the product now.
5. **Name past poker voters who are not on the roster.** The poker snapshot carries `PrincipalInfo` only for the
   roster (the team's members, `PokerStore.RosterSql`) and the present principals, so the table seats a past voter
   who is not a current member, such as an imported or earlier guest or a former member, as "Unknown" without a
   badge (`seatsOf` in `web/src/poker/model.js`, and the reveal and results piles). Carry `PrincipalInfo` for those
   voters, so their seats keep their name and the "guest" badge, as retro authors do (UX18 decision 3).
